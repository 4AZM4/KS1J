How a request for help is handled

No single person can approve help. The rule is enforced by the system itself, not just written down.

  1. 1

    You apply

    From the app or with a volunteer. Only the committee sees your details.

  2. 2

    A verifier checks

    Documents, need, and Sadaat lineage where it applies.

  3. 3

    A different trustee approves

    The person who verified can never approve the same case.

  4. 4

    Donors see the need

    Without your name, phone or address. Only what is needed and how much is left.

  5. 5

    The Jamaat pays directly

    To the hospital, school or family, with proof recorded.

Where your money goes

Each kind of giving has its own account. The system refuses a payment to the wrong place, so Sehme Sadaat can never reach a Non-Sadaat case and Sehme Imam can never reach an individual.

Payments are confirmed by the bank before they count, and records are never edited. A correction is a new entry everyone can trace.

Sehme Sadaat
Verified Sadaat (Syed) families only
Sehme Imam
Only institutions holding an ijazah from a Marja', never individuals
General donations
Any verified case
Loan repayments
Back into the fund for the next student
Lawajam
The Jamaat's own running costs, kept apart from everything else
